Frequently Asked Questions.

— 01 What makes Miles Masterclass different from a standard CLE webinar?
+
Most CLE is a recorded lecture with a quiz attached. Miles Masterclass is cinematic — scripted, shot, and edited like a streaming series, with practitioner-led narrative, AI workflows woven into every module, and Reels for when you only have 60 seconds between meetings. You earn credits the same way; the experience is nothing like it.
— 02 Will my CLE credits be reportable in my state?
+
Yes. Miles Education is a NASBA-registered sponsor (ID 149174) and we are pursuing accreditation in all 50 state bars. State boards have final authority on individual course acceptance — we publish state-by-state status as we go.
— 03 Do I really earn credits watching Reels under a minute?
+
Reels are not for credits — they are for momentum. Masterclasses earn the credits. Reels keep you sharp between them.
— 04 What does it cost — and what's your refund policy?
+
Pricing details are released with our public launch. Founding faculty cohort and early-access members receive lifetime preferred pricing. Cancellation and refund terms follow our published policy — straightforward and pro-rated.
— 05 I'm a practitioner — how do I become founding faculty?
+
Apply through the "Count Me In" link or have someone nominate you. Selected faculty get a script consultation, a full studio production day, and an equity-style revenue share on every credit earned through their masterclass.
— 06 Does it work offline / on the road / at 30,000 feet?
+
Mobile-first by design. Download masterclasses for offline viewing, sync watch progress when you reconnect, and pull certificates directly from the app.
